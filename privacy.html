Pawberry Island support

Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026. This policy covers Pawberry Island version 0.35 and its parent/teacher tools. Operator: Muhammed nedim Ipek, Lunara Mystic Studio. Contact: nedimiiipek@gmail.com.

No child account, tracking or advertising

The game does not require an account, a child's name, email address, precise location, camera, microphone or contact list. It has no chat, advertising identifier, ad network, analytics SDK, subscription or in-app purchase. We do not receive gameplay records from the app, sell child information or use it for advertising.

What stays on the device

Local storage holds game progress, earned stars and accessories, placed decorations, language/sound preferences, age level, completed activities and basic play counters. Parent/teacher tools can hold up to 30 anonymous profiles identified by an animal and colour, activity attempts, correct answers, help use, completion status, time spent and session dates. These are practice records, not diagnostic assessments. A parental PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as the entered PIN. These records remain on the device and are not automatically uploaded to us.

Backups, sharing and deletion

An adult may export a local backup or practice summary. Any saving or sharing destination is selected by that adult using the browser or device share sheet; the game does not automatically send the file to the developer. Keep exported files private. Local profiles can be removed in the adult tools. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove all local progress. We cannot restore records that were never sent to us. There is no cloud sync.

Offline files and voices

Native store packages include the game and recorded audio files. A web preview loads files from its host and may cache them for later offline play. The fixed synthetic voice recordings were produced during development: no microphone recording, live speech-generation service or child text is used during play. Adults can turn sound off.

Store and support services

Apple and Google process paid app purchases under their own policies. The game does not collect payment-card details. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; its provider may process normal connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. If an adult emails support, the email provider and operator process that message to answer the request. Do not include child information or backups. You may ask us to delete support correspondence that we control, subject to any required retention.

Children and changes

The game is designed for ages 3–7 with an adult-gated parent/teacher area. If you believe child information was sent to us, contact the support address so we can investigate and remove it where applicable. Any change to the game's data handling will be reflected in this policy and the relevant store disclosures.
