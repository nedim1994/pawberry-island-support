PAWBERRY ISLAND

A little island. A helping hand.

Support for parents and educators using Pawberry Island, a play-and-learn game for ages 3–7.

Contact

Email nedimiiipek@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model and a description of the problem. Please do not send a child's name, photo, classroom backup or other personal information.

Play and purchases

The store version is a single paid download. All 42 activities in 14 workshops are included, with no advertising, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Animal accessories are earned with play stars and do not cost real money.

English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Hindi and Indonesian are included. An adult can select the age level and review completed practice on the device. These records are not a developmental or medical assessment.

Progress and offline play

The Android and iOS store packages bundle the game files and recorded voices for offline play. Web previews may need a download before offline use. Progress stays on the device; there is no cloud account or automatic cross-device sync. Deleting the app or its data can delete progress. An adult can export and restore supported local backups in the parent/teacher area.
